KuiklyBase Datetime is an independently implemented minimal API in the
com.tencent.kuiklybase.datetime namespace. It does not incorporate the full
kotlinx-datetime source tree, timezone database parser, calendar model,
serialization layer, or kotlinx.datetime ABI. See PROVENANCE.md for the
audited divergence and per-platform patch catalog.
